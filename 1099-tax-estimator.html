How the estimate is calculated

  1. Self-employment tax: 92.35% of net profit is taxable. Social Security 12.4% applies up to the 2026 wage base of $184,500; Medicare 2.9% has no cap. The 0.9% Additional Medicare Tax applies above $200,000 (single) / $250,000 (joint). Total SE rate: 15.3%.
  2. Deduction: one-half of the SE tax is deductible from income, then the 2026 standard deduction ($16,100 single / $32,200 joint) is subtracted.
  3. Federal income tax: the 2026 marginal brackets (10%–37%) apply to the remaining taxable income. Rates are marginal — only the income inside each bracket is taxed at that rate.
  4. Quarterly payments: the total is split into four equal estimated payments.

Not included: state income tax, the Qualified Business Income (QBI) deduction (which can lower federal tax further for eligible filers), credits, and the 3.8% net investment income tax. Those vary by situation — see a tax professional for them.
