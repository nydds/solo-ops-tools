LLC Cost Compare

Formation and annual costs for a US LLC in Wyoming, Delaware, Florida, and New York — from each state's official fee schedule. Adjust the horizon and the New York assumptions to see the true multi-year cost.

New York requires publication in two county-designated newspapers for six weeks. Newspaper rates are set by the newspapers, not the state, so these are estimates by county tier — get a written quote before you commit.

The annual Form IT-204-LL fee is $25 up to $100,000 of NY-source gross income, then $50 / $175 / $500 / $1,500 / $3,000 / $4,500 in higher tiers. First-year LLCs pay a flat $25.

Wyoming's annual report fee is $60 minimum, or $0.0002 per $1 of assets located in Wyoming, whichever is greater. Leave at 0 for the $60 minimum.

What the numbers don't show

Form where you operate

The cheapest filing fee is rarely the cheapest total. If you form in Wyoming or Delaware but operate in your home state, you usually must also register there as a foreign LLC — and pay that state's fees too. For most solo founders, forming in the state where you actually work is the simpler and often cheaper path.

New York publication is the swing factor

New York's $200 filing fee looks moderate, but the mandatory six-week newspaper publication can cost $200 in a low-cost upstate county or $1,000–$1,900+ in Manhattan — set by the newspapers, not the state. Choose the county tier above to see how much it moves the total.
