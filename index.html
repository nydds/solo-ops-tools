Frequently asked questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. There is no account, no paywall, and no charge. The site is supported by affiliate links to the tools we recommend.

Do the estimates include state tax?

Not as a rule. The 1099 and quarterly estimators cover federal tax only. The S-corp calculator adds the S-corp-specific minimums for California, New York, New Jersey and Florida, but no state income tax anywhere — check your state's rules.

Should I elect S-corp status?

That is a question for a CPA, not a calculator — but the calculator tells you what the election would cost and save at your numbers, and the profit level where it breaks even. Below roughly $70,000–$100,000 of profit it usually costs more than it saves.

Is this tax advice?

No. Results are planning estimates based on 2026 figures. Tax situations vary; for decisions that affect your return, consult a licensed tax professional.
