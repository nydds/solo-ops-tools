About Solo Ops Tools

Solo Ops Tools is a small, free set of calculators for US solopreneurs, freelancers, and single-member LLCs — people who run a business of one and need straight answers about tax and formation costs.

What this site does

  • 1099 Tax Estimator — estimates 2026 self-employment tax and federal income tax for 1099 income, plus quarterly estimated payments.
  • S-Corp vs LLC Calculator — compares a sole proprietor or single-member LLC against an S-corp election: payroll tax saved on distributions, the added costs, and the break-even profit.
  • Quarterly Tax Calculator — the 2026 Form 1040-ES installment amounts by due date, with the safe harbor floor and a penalty-avoidance checklist.
  • LLC Cost Compare — compares formation and annual costs for an LLC in Wyoming, Delaware, Florida, and New York over 1–10 years.

How figures are sourced

Every rate and fee on this site is checked against an official source for 2026: IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32 for federal brackets, standard deductions and §199A thresholds, the Social Security Administration for the $184,500 wage base, IRS Form 1040-ES and Form 2210 for estimated-tax dates and payment rules, state revenue department minimum-tax schedules for S-corp costs, and each state's Secretary of State fee schedule for LLC costs. The full list, with the exact figures, is in SOURCES.md. If a figure cannot be verified, it is not used.

What this site is not

This site is not tax advice, legal advice, or a substitute for a licensed professional. Results are planning estimates. Tax law and state fees change; verify current figures on the official agency site before you file or form an entity. We do not provide individual tax or legal advice, and we do not review your numbers.
