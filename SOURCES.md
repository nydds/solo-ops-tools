## Federal income tax (IRS Revenue Procedure 2025-32, published Oct 9, 2025)
- Standard deduction: Single $16,100 · Married filing jointly $32,200 · Head of household $24,150 · Married filing separately $16,100.
- Single brackets (taxable income): 10% $0–$12,400 · 12% $12,400–$50,400 · 22% $50,400–$105,700 · 24% $105,700–$201,775 · 32% $201,775–$256,225 · 35% $256,225–$640,600 · 37% over $640,600.
- MFJ brackets (taxable income): 10% $0–$24,800 · 12% $24,800–$100,800 · 22% $100,800–$211,400 · 24% $211,400–$403,550 · 32% $403,550–$512,450 · 35% $512,450–$768,700 · 37% over $768,700.
- Cross-checked against IRS news release IR-2025-103 and multiple independent summaries of Rev. Proc. 2025-32 (all agree).

## Self-employment tax (IRS / SSA)
- SE tax rate 15.3% = Social Security 12.4% + Medicare 2.9% (Schedule SE).
- Taxable base = 92.35% of net self-employment earnings.
- 2026 Social Security wage base (contribution and benefit base): $184,500 — Social Security Administration, announced Oct 24, 2025. Max Social Security portion for a self-employed person: $22,878 (12.4% × $184,500). Medicare has no wage cap.
- Additional Medicare tax 0.9% on earnings over $200,000 (single) / $250,000 (married filing jointly) — IRS.
- One-half of SE tax is deductible from income when computing federal income tax (above-the-line deduction).

## Estimated tax payments (IRS Form 1040-ES, 2026)
- Due dates for tax year 2026: April 15, 2026 · June 15, 2026 · September 15, 2026 · January 15, 2027.
- You may skip the January 15, 2027 payment if you file your 2026 return and pay the full balance by February 1, 2027.

## LLC state fees (state Secretary of State fee schedules, verified 2026)
- Wyoming: Articles of Organization $100 · Annual report $60 minimum (or $0.0002 per $1 of assets located in Wyoming, whichever is greater) · No franchise tax · No state individual income tax.
- Delaware: Certificate of Formation $90 · Annual franchise tax $300/year (flat for LLCs).
- Florida: Articles of Organization $125 · Annual report $138.75/year.
- New York: Articles of Organization $200 · Biennial Statement $9 every 2 years · Certificate of Publication $50 · Mandatory newspaper publication (LLC Law § 206): two county-designated newspapers, once a week for six successive weeks, within 120 days — newspaper charges are set by the newspapers, not the state (roughly $200 in low-cost upstate counties to $1,000–$1,900+ in Manhattan) · Annual LLC filing fee Form IT-204-LL on prior-year NY-source gross income: $25 (≤$100,000), $50 ($100,001–$250,000), $175 ($250,001–$500,000), $500 ($500,001–$1,000,000), $1,500 ($1,000,001–$5,000,000), $3,000 ($5,000,001–$25,000,000), $4,500 (over $25,000,000); disregarded single-member LLCs pay a flat $25. No franchise tax for LLCs.

## Notes / limits (what this site does NOT do)
- No state income tax is included in the 1099 estimator (federal only).
- No Qualified Business Income (QBI) deduction is applied — it can lower federal tax further for eligible filers.
- NY publication costs are estimates by county tier, not official quotes; the state does not set newspaper rates.
- Registered-agent fees are not included (they are third-party services, not state fees).
- All results are estimates for planning only — not tax or legal advice.
